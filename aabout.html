Who We Are

Founded on principles of execution excellence and absolute cargo transparency, Airsell Cargo has expanded into a leading freight forwarding organization connecting East African trade gateways to global commercial hubs across Asia, Europe, and the Middle East.

From full container sea freight (FCL) passing through Berbera Port to time-critical air charters descending into Hargeisa International Airport, our integrated dispatch network handles end-to-end supply chain demands with precision.

  • Licensed Airport & Seaport Customs Clearance
  • Direct Air Consolidation Routes from Dubai, Sharjah & Jeddah
  • Dedicated Fleet for Secured Overland Hinterland Transit
  • Complete Commercial & Regulatory Manifest Compliance
