About Airsell Cargo

Airsell Cargo is a premier multimodal logistics and freight forwarding provider headquartered in Hargeisa, Somaliland. Founded in 2024, we empower regional and international trade by seamlessly connecting local commerce with global markets through key trade gateways, including Hargeisa Airport and the Port of Berbera. Driven by technology and operational excellence, we deliver end-to-end supply chain transparency, cloud-based tracking, and digital manifest integration—ensuring secure, efficient, and reliable cargo movement across East Africa and beyond.

Core Operational Capabilities

We provide comprehensive end-to-end logistics solutions designed to optimize your supply chain and keep global freight moving smoothly.

Air Freight Forwarding

Expedited, reliable air transport solutions routed seamlessly via Hargeisa Airport for time-sensitive cargo and high-value shipments.

Sea Freight Forwarding

Full Container Load (FCL) and Less-than-Container Load (LCL) consolidation services through the strategic deep-water Port of Berbera.

Multimodal Supply Chain

Customized land, sea, and air integration for streamlined end-to-end freight distribution across regional and international borders.
