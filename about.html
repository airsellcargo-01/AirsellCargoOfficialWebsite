Who We Are

Headquartered in Hargeisa, Airsell Cargo operates at the intersection of international trade corridors and local supply chains. With operational footholds at Hargeisa Egal International Airport (HGA) and Berbera Deep Sea Port (BBO), we facilitate seamless trade between Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and East Africa.

Whether handling urgent air shipments, bulk ocean containers, or complex customs clearance, our team ensures your cargo moves safely, transparently, and on time.
