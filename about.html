About Airsell Cargo

Connecting Somaliland and Africa to global trade routes through reliable, technology-driven logistics solutions.

Home / About Us

Company Overview & Mission

Established in 2024, Airsell Cargo is a licensed freight forwarder based in Hargeisa, Somaliland. We specialize in connecting Somaliland and the broader African market to global trade channels via reliable, scalable, and high-performance logistics.

Our Mission: To deliver seamless cargo movement with absolute transparency, operating efficiency, and enduring client trust.

Our Strategic Operational Hubs

Our operations are anchored directly across key multi-modal gateways in the region, ensuring fluid transition between air and sea vectors:

Hargeisa Airport Hub (HGA)

Optimized for rapid air freight forwarding, courier dispatch, express parcel sorting, and priority regional distribution.

Berbera Port Hub (BBO)

Anchored for containerized ocean freight forwarding, bulk cargo handling, and robust sea-to-land multimodal connections.

Technology & Global Compliance

We combine on-the-ground capability with modern digital architecture, providing advanced tracking options via XML, EDI, and API integration to support institutional partners and enterprise clients seamlessly.

Accreditations & Licensing

Licensed by the Somaliland Civil Aviation Authority, IATA Cargolink Directory member, and FIATA individual member.

Quality Standards

Committed to QAHE & CIS International Quality Standards to guarantee audit readiness and rigorous compliance protocols.

Partner With Us

Whether you require perishable handling, oversized cargo solutions, or custom brokerage support backed by 24/7 customer care, our team is ready to optimize your supply chain.
