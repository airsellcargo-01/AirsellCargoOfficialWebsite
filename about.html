About Airsell Cargo

Headquartered in Hargeisa, Somaliland, Airsell Cargo is a premier multimodal freight forwarder connecting East Africa to international trade networks. We specialize in air cargo, ocean freight, and customs clearance via Hargeisa Airport and Berbera Port—delivering end-to-end supply chain transparency through cloud-based manifest tracking.

Why Partner With Airsell Cargo

Built on international freight standards, local operational mastery, and modern digital logistics.

Air Freight Leadership

Scheduled air cargo operations and express consolidation routed seamlessly via Hargeisa Airport for time-critical regional and international shipments.

Maritime Gateway

Full Container Load (FCL) and LCL ocean freight solutions leveraging Berbera Port’s deep-water commercial hub to connect global shipping lines.

Digital Compliance

Direct digital customs clearance brokerage, duty assessment, and cloud tracking engineered to eliminate supply chain friction and delay.

Global Industry Credentials

Our freight operations comply strictly with international civil aviation and maritime logistics standards.
